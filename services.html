Service 01

Water Filtration Systems

Your tap water travels a long distance before it reaches your glass — picking up chlorine, sediment, heavy metals, and other contaminants along the way. A Trusty Water filtration system removes those impurities at the point of entry or use, so every faucet in your home delivers clean, great-tasting water.

What We Install

  • Whole-Home Systems — Filter every drop at the main line. Great for chlorine, sediment, and taste issues.
  • Reverse Osmosis (RO) — Under-sink system that removes up to 99% of dissolved contaminants. Best for drinking and cooking water.
  • Carbon Block Filters — Removes chlorine, chloramines, VOCs, and bad taste and odor.
  • UV Purification — Kills bacteria and viruses without chemicals. Ideal for well water.
  • Filter Replacements & Maintenance — Scheduled service to keep your system performing perfectly.

Service 02

Water Softener Systems

Hard water — water high in calcium and magnesium — is one of the most common water problems in the country. It silently damages pipes, wears out appliances faster, and causes the dry skin and scale buildup that homeowners notice every day. A properly sized water softener solves all of it.

Softener Options We Offer

  • Salt-Based Ion Exchange — The gold standard. Completely removes hardness minerals for silky-soft water throughout your home.
  • Salt-Free Conditioners — Changes mineral structure to prevent scale without removing minerals. Good for low-sodium households.
  • Iron Filters — Removes iron and manganese that cause rust stains and metallic taste.
  • Combination Systems — Softener + filtration in one unit for comprehensive treatment.
  • Salt Delivery Service — Regular salt top-ups delivered and loaded, so you never have to think about maintenance.
