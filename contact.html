Frequently Asked Questions

Is the water test really free?

Yes, completely free. There is zero cost for the in-home water test and zero obligation to purchase anything. We test your water, explain the results, and if you want a recommendation we'll give you one — but the test itself is always free.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-home filtration and softener installations take 2–4 hours. Under-sink reverse osmosis systems typically take 1–2 hours. We'll give you a time estimate when we schedule and do our best to stay within it.

Do you offer financing?

Ask us about financing options when we do your water test consultation. We work with several financing partners to make water treatment accessible for every household and budget.

Do I need to be home during the water test?

Yes — we need access to your water supply, and we'll want to go over the results with you in person. The test takes about 20 minutes and we'll work around your schedule.
