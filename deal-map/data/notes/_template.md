# <Customer> — raw notes

Dump everything here. Prose, fragments, bullets, a pasted CRM export, a transcript of you
talking it through out loud — it does not need to be tidy. The `deal-story-coach` agent reads
this file and writes `data/deals/<slug>.json` from it.

Answer what you know. Leave the rest blank — blanks are useful, they show where the story is
thin, and the coach will ask rather than invent. **Never let anything in here be made up: every
number in the deck traces back to something you write in this file.**

---

## The account

- Who are they, in one line? (industry, size, what they actually do)
- What were they using before? How long had that been in place?
- How did this start — outbound, inbound, referral, expansion?
- What made this deal harder than a normal one?

## The pain

- What was broken, in the customer's own words?
- **The number.** What was it costing them — hours, dollars, headcount, days to close, risk?
  Where did that number come from, and who gave it to you?
- What was the consequence of doing nothing? (an audit, a board commitment, a deadline, a churn risk)

## Where it went wrong

Be honest here — this is the part that makes the story credible.

- When did it stall, go dark, or nearly die?
- What did you get wrong? What would you do differently?
- Did you lose a champion, miss a buyer, or misread the process?

## The turn

- What specific move changed the deal? A meeting, a reframe, a person you finally got to,
  a number you put in front of someone?
- Why did that work?
